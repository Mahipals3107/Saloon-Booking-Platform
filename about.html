Get to know us

At Style & Scissors, we are dedicated to creating a grooming experience that combines modern style with genuine care. Our salon brings together skilled professionals, quality services, and a welcoming atmosphere for every client.

From everyday grooming to special occasions, we focus on understanding your needs and creating a look that suits your personality. Every service is delivered with attention to detail, comfort, and professionalism. Our goal is simple — to make every visit enjoyable and leave you feeling confident, refreshed, and ready to shine.

Founder Story

Rajiv Sharma

Rajiv Sharma founded Style & Scissors with a passion for grooming, creativity, and making people feel confident. What started as a simple vision has grown into a space where modern style meets personalized care. With years of experience in the beauty and grooming industry, Rajiv believes that every client deserves individual attention and an experience that feels comfortable and welcoming. His focus on quality, professionalism, and continuous learning has helped shape the values of Style & Scissors.

His vision is to create a salon where people don't just come for a service, but leave feeling refreshed, confident, and happy with their look.
